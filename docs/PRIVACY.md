# Rugby Timer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Rugby Timer does not create an app account, display advertising, use analytics, make network requests, or send personal data to the developer.

When the user starts a match recording, the app asks the Garmin device platform to record location, distance, and available heart-rate samples in a local Garmin FIT activity. The app also writes team-relative rugby events, match time, period, and scores into that activity where the device supports Connect IQ developer fields. Garmin controls synchronization of saved activities to Garmin Connect and the user's associated retention and deletion controls.

The app stores match configuration and a compact active-match recovery checkpoint on the watch. This may include team-relative match events and cumulative distance, but not player names, contact details, account identifiers, or precise route history. Resetting a match removes the app-owned active-match checkpoint. Saved FIT activities remain under the user's Garmin device and Garmin Connect controls.

Rugby Timer does not sell, rent, transfer, or independently retain user information outside the user's Garmin environment.

Privacy questions or deletion issues concerning app-owned data may be reported through the project's issue tracker: https://github.com/alihelkhatib/garmin-rugby-timer-v2/issues

Garmin services and devices are governed by Garmin's own privacy notices and controls. Data handled by Garmin is submitted to Garmin, not to the Rugby Timer developer.
